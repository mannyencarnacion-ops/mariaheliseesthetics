About

Hi, I’m Mariah

I’m a licensed esthetician working out of my own room inside Mnandi Salon & Spa in Leominster. I built this practice around one idea: most people have never had anyone actually look at their skin and explain what is going on with it.

So that is where every appointment starts. A skin analysis, a plain-English explanation, and a treatment chosen for the skin in front of me. If a consultation is the honest answer before a peel, that is what I will tell you.
