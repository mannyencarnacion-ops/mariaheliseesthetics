About

Skin first. Then the treatment.

I’m Mariah, a licensed esthetician in Leominster, Massachusetts.

Most people book a facial the way you order off a menu — you pick the one that sounds nicest and hope it is the right one. I would rather look at your skin first. Every appointment starts with a cleanse and a proper analysis, and then I choose the treatment for what I actually see.

That is also why the consultation exists as its own bookable appointment. Fifty minutes, $45, and you leave understanding your own skin and what would genuinely help it. Plenty of people book it, go away with a plan, and come back later. That is a good outcome.

Alongside facials and peels I do brows, lashes, waxing and makeup. Brows get mapped to your face rather than a trend. Waxing is unhurried and nothing is ever double-dipped. Makeup gets done for the day it matters — and if you would rather learn to do it yourself, that is a lesson you can book.

What to expect

Your first appointment

Before you arrive

Come with clean skin if you can, and bring whatever you currently use — or a photo of the labels. Skip retinol for a few days before a peel.

In the room

Pre-cleanse, cleanse, and a proper look at your skin under a lamp. I will tell you what I see before I decide what to do about it.

Afterwards

You get aftercare in plain language and an honest answer on timing. Skin takes cycles, not appointments — I will tell you if something needs more than one visit.
